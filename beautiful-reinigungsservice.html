01

Project Overview

The Idea

The goal was to create a modern and trustworthy online presence for a local cleaning service. Customers should be able to understand the available services quickly and contact the company directly.

My Role

I designed and implemented the website from scratch, created the responsive user interface, integrated the contact form and deployed the final website online using Cloudflare Pages.

02

Main Features

Responsive Design

Optimized layouts for desktop, tablet and mobile devices.

Service Overview

Clear presentation of cleaning services with expandable service information.

Contact Form

Contact requests are processed securely through Formspree without leaving the website.

Direct Contact

Customers can contact the company directly by phone, email or WhatsApp.

Service Selection

When a customer chooses a service, the contact form automatically selects it.

Legal Pages

Dedicated Impressum and privacy policy pages were included for the German market.

03

Security

Security was considered during development and deployment. The project uses HTTPS and security headers through Cloudflare Pages.

  • Content Security Policy (CSP)
  • X-Frame-Options
  • X-Content-Type-Options
  • Referrer Policy
  • Permissions Policy
  • Honeypot spam protection for the contact form
  • No passwords or API secrets stored in frontend code
